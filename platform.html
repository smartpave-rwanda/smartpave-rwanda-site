The SMART-PAVE digital environment

From pavement data to engineering decisions.

SMART-PAVE Rwanda is developing an integrated engineering platform that brings traffic intelligence, pavement response analysis, deformation monitoring and geospatial information into one digital environment.

Platform capabilities

An integrated environment for pavement intelligence.

The platform is being developed around connected engineering capabilities that help organize information, analyse pavement behaviour and support infrastructure monitoring.

01 / TRAFFIC

Traffic Intelligence

Organize vehicle passage information, traffic counts, vehicle classes, AADT and provisional ESAL analysis.

02 / PAVEMENT

Pavement Response

Explore multilayer pavement response calculations and engineering indicators under defined loading assumptions.

03 / DEFORMATION

Deformation Monitoring

Organize deflection, residual deformation and settlement measurements for comparison over time.

04 / GEOINTELLIGENCE

Geospatial Monitoring

Connect road sections, chainage and geographic coordinates with engineering observations.

05 / ANALYTICS

Data Analysis

Bring traffic and pavement information together to explore trends, indicators and possible maintenance priorities.

06 / DECISION SUPPORT

Engineering Insights

Present organized information through dashboards and reports to support engineering review and investigation.
