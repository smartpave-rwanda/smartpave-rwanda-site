About the project

Turning pavement information into engineering insight.

Road infrastructure needs reliable information about traffic loading, pavement behaviour and changing road conditions. SMART-PAVE aims to bring these areas together through an integrated digital engineering platform.

The project is being developed in Rwanda, with a focus on practical pavement monitoring, analytical tools and geospatial decision support.

Analyse Explore pavement response, loading and deformation indicators.

Technology and capabilities

An integrated approach to pavement intelligence.

The platform brings together several engineering and information streams in one digital environment.

01 / TRAFFIC

Traffic Monitoring

Organise vehicle passage information, traffic counts and vehicle-class data for road analysis.

02 / LOADING

ESAL Analysis

Explore equivalent standard axle loading indicators to support pavement traffic assessment.

03 / STRUCTURAL

Pavement Response

Explore multilayer pavement response under applied loading using engineering analysis models.

04 / DEFORMATION

Deformation Analysis

Organise deflection, residual deformation and settlement measurements for trend analysis.

05 / GEOSPATIAL

GIS and Location Intelligence

Relate road sections, chainage and location information to pavement monitoring data.

06 / INSIGHT

Engineering Decision Support

Bring monitoring outputs and analytical indicators together to support engineering review.
