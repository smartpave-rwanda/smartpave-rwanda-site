Integrated engineering modules

Six connected technology capabilities.

SMART-PAVE is being developed as a modular platform. Each capability contributes a different part of the pavement monitoring and analysis workflow.

MODULE 01

Traffic and vehicle monitoring

Capture vehicle passage events, timestamps, road-section references and available vehicle information. The platform is designed to support simulated inputs and future sensor integration.

Traffic data acquisition
MODULE 02

Traffic volume and classification

Organise vehicle observations by class and time period to support traffic counts, hourly or daily summaries and Average Annual Daily Traffic analysis when sufficient representative data is available.

AADT · Vehicle classes
MODULE 03

Equivalent axle loading

Translate eligible vehicle passages into equivalent standard axle loading estimates using documented vehicle-class factors and selected loading assumptions.

ESAL · Cumulative loading
MODULE 04

Multilayer pavement response

Explore the response of a flexible pavement represented by asphalt, granular base, subbase and subgrade layers under defined tyre or axle loading conditions.

Layered elastic analysis
MODULE 05

Deflection and deformation

Organise elastic deflection, residual deformation and settlement observations over time, with the aim of supporting pavement response assessment and future condition-trend analysis.

Pavement response monitoring
MODULE 06

GIS and engineering intelligence

Associate observations with road sections, chainage and geographic coordinates to support spatial visualisation, location-based review, alerts and engineering decisions.

GIS · Decision support
01 / Traffic intelligence

Understand the traffic using the road.

Traffic observations provide the operational context for pavement assessment. SMART-PAVE is designed to organise vehicle passage records and connect traffic activity to specific road sections.

Vehicle passage recordsTimestamp, vehicle class, source and road-section association.
Traffic volume indicatorsCounts and time-based summaries. AADT requires representative survey coverage and appropriate expansion methods.
Equivalent standard axle loadingESAL estimates based on selected class factors and loading assumptions, subject to engineering review.
02 / Pavement mechanics

Explore the response of flexible pavement layers.

The multilayer analysis capability represents a pavement as a system of material layers with defined thicknesses and elastic properties. A load is applied and the resulting response is evaluated under the model assumptions.

Layer properties: thickness · elastic modulus · Poisson's ratio
Asphalt layerSurface layer represented by its thickness and assumed material properties.
Granular base and subbaseStructural support layers represented through their elastic properties and thicknesses.
SubgradeFoundation layer influencing the response of the pavement structure.
Load-response evaluationExplore calculated response for defined axle or tyre loading scenarios.
03 / Pavement condition

Track pavement response over time.

SMART-PAVE includes a data structure for recording different pavement measurement types. Keeping these measurements distinct is essential for meaningful engineering interpretation.

Elastic deflectionRecoverable vertical pavement movement under an applied load, recorded in millimetres.
Residual deformationPermanent deformation remaining after the applied load is removed.
SettlementVertical movement of the pavement or supporting ground relative to a defined reference.
Trend analysisCompare compatible measurements by location, measurement type, loading and source before interpreting changes over time.
04 / Spatial intelligence

Connect engineering observations to location.

Spatial context helps engineers understand where traffic events and pavement measurements occur. SMART-PAVE is designed to associate records with road sections, chainage and geographic coordinates.

Road-section mappingOrganise monitored locations into identifiable road sections.
Chainage referencingRelate observations to positions along a road alignment.
Location intelligenceReview events, measurement histories and potential areas requiring engineering investigation.
05 / Engineering decisions

Turn organised data into useful engineering insights.

The platform brings together traffic, pavement response and location information to support review and prioritisation. It is intended to assist engineering judgement, not replace professional assessment.

Monitoring indicatorsPresent available observations, status indicators and data-quality information.
Historical comparisonReview changes in traffic and pavement measurements over defined periods.
Engineering reviewIdentify records and locations that may require further inspection or field investigation.
Engineering and validation note: SMART-PAVE is an evolving prototype. Simulated data, provisional traffic factors and model-based calculations must be clearly distinguished from sensor measurements and validated field results. Engineering outputs require suitable calibration, representative data, documented assumptions and review by qualified professionals before use in design or maintenance decisions.
